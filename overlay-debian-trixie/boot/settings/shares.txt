## Network shares: music on a computer or NAS, for services such as Lyrion Music Server.

# Each share is mounted, read-only, in its own folder in /media/music, where services that play
# music look. You can add and remove them on the screen, in Settings, under Services, or here:
# a block of lines for each share, with a blank line between them.
#
#   share=//server/folder    the share, as \\server\folder or //server/folder, with the server's
#                            address or its name
#   username=                its user name and password, or leave both empty for a guest share
#   password=
#
# For example:
#
#   share=//192.168.1.20/Music
#   username=music
#   password=secret
